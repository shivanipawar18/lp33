SURVEY REPORT: TYPES OF BLOCKCHAINS AND THEIR REAL-TIME USE CASES

1. INTRODUCTION
Blockchain is a decentralized digital ledger technology used to store information securely. It records transactions in blocks connected using cryptography. Once data is recorded, it is difficult to modify.

2. TYPES OF BLOCKCHAINS

1. Public Blockchain
- Open to everyone.
- Anyone can participate in transactions.
- Highly transparent and decentralized.
- Examples: Bitcoin and Ethereum.
- Use case: Cryptocurrency transactions.

2. Private Blockchain
- Controlled by a single organization.
- Only authorized users can access it.
- Provides privacy and faster transactions.
- Example: Hyperledger Fabric.
- Use case: Banking and company record management.

3. Consortium Blockchain
- Managed by multiple organizations.
- Access is limited to selected members.
- Supports collaboration between organizations.
- Example: R3 Corda.
- Use case: Supply chain management and interbank transactions.

4. Hybrid Blockchain
- Combines public and private blockchain features.
- Some information is public, while sensitive data remains private.
- Provides flexibility and privacy.
- Example: Dragonchain.
- Use case: Healthcare and government services.

3. REAL-TIME USE CASES OF BLOCKCHAIN
1. Banking: Secure money transfers and international payments.
2. Healthcare: Secure storage and sharing of patient medical records.
3. Supply Chain: Tracking products from manufacturers to customers.
4. Education: Verification of certificates and academic records.
5. Voting: Supporting secure and transparent digital voting systems.
6. Real Estate: Managing property ownership records and transactions.
7. Cryptocurrency: Digital payments using Bitcoin and Ethereum.
8. Insurance: Automating claim processing using smart contracts.

4. ADVANTAGES OF BLOCKCHAIN
- High security.
- Transparency.
- Decentralization.
- Reduced risk of fraud.
- Better data management.
- No central authority is required in decentralized systems.

5. CONCLUSION
Blockchain is an emerging technology that provides secure, transparent and reliable data management. Its applications in banking, healthcare, education and supply chains make it useful in many real-world situations.
